1 Summary

Volkovoice is a Russian ↔ English voice translator. Most of what it does happens on your phone, and you decide which optional features to switch on.

Speech & translation stay local Once the Russian and English language packs are downloaded, translation runs on your device without a connection.
History is encrypted Saved turns are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Cloud sync is off unless you sign in and enable it.
Voice samples never leave the app A recording in Voice Studio stays in app-private storage and does not create a cloned voice.
Supported by ads Ads are served by Google AdMob. You can change your ad choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not collect your name, contacts, calendar, precise location, photos or health data.

5 Speech recognition and spoken output

Recognition

Volkovoice does not contain its own speech recogniser. It hands audio to the recognition service Android has configured on your device, which is usually provided by Google or by your device manufacturer. That service may process the audio on your device or transmit it to its own provider, under that provider's privacy policy — not ours. Volkovoice does not save or upload your recordings.

The app asks for your confirmation before the first continuous-listening session, because that mode keeps the microphone active until you stop it.

Spoken output

Translations are spoken by the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Some voices are marked as "network"; if the engine reports that it needs a connection, the app shows a separate confirmation before sending any text to it. Without such a consent the app uses an on-device voice or stays silent.

6 Translation on your device

Translation is performed by Google ML Kit's on-device translation models. Downloaded Russian and English language packs are stored in app-private storage. Downloading a pack requires a network connection (Wi-Fi by preference); the text you translate is processed locally and is not sent to Google for translation. Removing the packs in Settings → Local data reclaims that storage.

7 Your optional account

You can use Volkovoice without an account. If you create one, we use Google Firebase Authentication to process your email address and authentication details (such as a hashed credential and verification status). We use it only to sign you in, to secure your account and, if you switch it on, to sync conversation turns between your own devices.

  • We never ask for your name, phone number or postal address to create an account.
  • Synced turns are stored in your record, protected by Firestore security rules so that other users cannot read them.
  • Cloud sync is off by default and can be turned off again at any time.

Deleting your account

Two ways to delete it.
1. In the app (fastest): open Volkovoice → Settings → Account → Delete Firebase account → re-enter your password → confirm. The account is removed immediately.
2. By email: write to [email protected] from the address on the account, with the subject Account deletion request. We may ask you to confirm from that same address; verified requests are completed within 30 days.
What is deleted: the authentication record (email, credentials, verification status) and any conversation turns synced to it. What is not: data stored only on your device — erase that with Settings → Local data → Delete all local data, or by uninstalling the app. Temporary deactivation or "freezing" is never offered in place of deletion.

You can also request deletion without the app. You may also request deletion without the app using our address below, or follow the two steps in this section — you can also write to [email protected]. We delete the authentication record and any synced turns within 30 days of a verified request. Deleting the account does not delete data stored only on your device — clear that in the app or by uninstalling it.

8 Advertising

Volkovoice is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. The app shows a banner on the main screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after a translation is completed. Interstitials are rate limited (at least three translations apart and at least 60 seconds between them) and never appear while you are listening, signing in, recording a voice sample or inside Settings.

  • Consent first. Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), the app runs Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow before any ad is requested. If you decline personalised advertising, ads are still shown but are non-personalised.
  • What the ads SDK may access. Your device's advertising ID, IP address, coarse/derived location from the IP, and app and device information, in order to deliver and cap ads, measure their performance and detect invalid traffic. Google describes this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Change your choice. Settings → Ad privacy options reopens the consent form whenever your region requires one. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share personal data with advertisers beyond what the ads SDK does as described above.
Because Volkovoice is not directed at children, ads are not configured for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child is using the app, see section 13.

9 Other providers you choose to enable

Two integrations are disabled unless you configure them:

  • A third-party voice provider (for example ElevenLabs). If you paste an API key in Settings, text and any voice sample you use with that feature are sent to that provider under its terms and privacy policy; the key is stored in app-private preferences on your device. Without a key this feature stays off.
  • Cloud sync through Firebase, described in section 7.

If you enable voice cloning with a third-party provider, you are responsible for having the speaker's permission and for complying with that provider's terms.

11 Your rights

  • Correction — have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure — have your account and synced data deleted.

Most data lives only on your device, so the quickest erasure is in the app or by uninstalling. For anything we hold on our side, email [email protected] — we answer within 30 days. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before acting.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising outside the consent choices described in section 8, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Nigeria: we process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

12 Security

  • Conversation history is encrypted with AES-GCM using a random key stored in the Android Keystore. The key is non-exportable and never leaves your device.
  • Sensitive files are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS, and Firestore access is restricted by security rules so that an account can only read its own record.
  • No system is perfect. If you find a vulnerability, please report it to [email protected] rather than disclosing it publicly, and we will investigate.

13 Children

Volkovoice is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us at [email protected] and we will delete any associated data.

14 International transfers
